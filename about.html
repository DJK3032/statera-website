About Statera Solutions

We are a defense technology advisory firm built by operators — bridging the gap between commercial innovation and the complex realities of federal adoption.

Our Mission

Statera Solutions was founded on a straightforward premise: too many capable technology companies struggle to navigate the federal market — not because their solutions aren't strong, but because the path to adoption is uniquely complex.

We exist to close that gap. Our team combines deep operational experience across the Department of Defense, intelligence community, and federal civilian agencies with a practical understanding of how startups and growth-stage companies actually work.

The result is advisory that's grounded in how things actually get done — from shaping requirements and navigating procurement to building the relationships that open doors.
