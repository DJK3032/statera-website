Daniel Kurtenbach

Founder, Statera Solutions. An operator who has run a defense technology company and built hardware operations at scale.

Background

Most technical founders I meet are excellent at the product and stuck on everything around it. That was my job as COO of Armaments Research Company, a defense technology company building AI-enabled weapon sensors, where I managed the P&L of a business doing about $10 million a year and led its hiring.

Before that I worked in emerging hardware operations at Apple and in DataRobot's defense and intelligence practice, and served as Chief Growth Officer of the Krach Institute for Tech Diplomacy. I was a White House Fellow from 2020 to 2021, in the Office of the Vice President and at the Small Business Administration.

I spent nine years as an Army Infantry and then Special Forces officer, including work in U.S. embassies with Special Operations Command.

Statera Solutions is how I do this work now: fixed-term operating builds for technical founders, and federal capture for commercial companies entering the government market.
