01

Startup Advisory

From product-market fit to government-market fit.

Building a great product is only half the challenge. For startups targeting the federal space, the other half is understanding how government buys, what it values, and how to position your company as a credible partner — not just another vendor.

Go-to-Market Strategy

Develop a phased federal market entry plan — identifying target agencies, procurement vehicles, and initial beachhead opportunities that build credibility.

Pitch & Positioning

Translate your commercial value proposition into the language of federal requirements. Help you tell a story that resonates with government buyers and evaluators.

Partnership & Teaming

Identify and facilitate introductions to prime contractors, system integrators, and channel partners who can accelerate your path to contract.

Growth Planning

Scale intelligently — from first pilot to multi-agency presence. We help you build the internal capabilities, processes, and relationships needed for sustainable federal revenue.

02

Defense Technology Consulting

Your guide to the federal landscape.

The defense market has its own rules, its own language, and its own pace. We help companies understand the terrain — from procurement vehicles and compliance frameworks to the unwritten norms that determine whether a technology gets adopted or shelved.

Market Landscape Analysis

Map the competitive environment, identify whitespace opportunities, and understand where your solution fits within the existing ecosystem of programs and contracts.

Procurement Navigation

Demystify SBIR/STTR, OTAs, GWACs, and other vehicles — helping you choose the right path to get your technology in front of the right buyers.

Compliance Readiness

Understand the compliance landscape — from FedRAMP to security certifications — and develop a practical path toward meeting the requirements that matter for your target contracts.

Stakeholder Engagement

Build relationships with the program managers, end users, and decision-makers who matter — through targeted engagement strategies grounded in understanding of the customer's priorities.

03

Systems Integration

The right team for the technical challenge.

Federal environments run on a patchwork of legacy and modern systems. We manage integration efforts by assembling and coordinating the right technical specialists — ensuring solutions work within the constraints of real-world government IT while keeping projects on track and on mission.

Requirements & Architecture Planning

Work with your team and ours to assess existing system landscapes, define integration requirements, and develop an architecture that aligns with agency modernization priorities.

Technical Team Coordination

Assemble and manage the right engineering talent for the specific integration challenge — whether that's bridging legacy infrastructure, API development, or data migration.

Technical Positioning

Help your team articulate integration capabilities in the language of federal acquisitions — aligning technical strengths with program requirements.

Deployment Oversight

Manage the transition from proof-of-concept to production — coordinating across security frameworks, stakeholder reviews, and operational readiness milestones.
